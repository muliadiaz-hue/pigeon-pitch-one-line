One line, 200 characters at most: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup. A judge model reads the lines in pairs and the funniest wins the reward.

Entry — "Statue Subscribers" (163 characters):

> Our customers are statues: they never cancel, never complain, and pay on time in breadcrumbs — the only subscription business where churn is physically impossible.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` to `/output` (set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to override). The program validates the pitch against the 1–200 character gate and the single-line rule before writing. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
